About Scheduled Syncs
  • Schedules automatically run "Initial Sync Only" mode (Schema + Data)
  • All configured slave databases will be synced simultaneously
  • Two easy scheduling options:
Time Delay:

Run every X hours or days

Examples: Every 12 hours • Every 2 days • Every 6 hours

Exact Time:

Run at a specific time with frequency options

Examples: Daily at 15:30 • Weekdays at 09:00 • Every Monday at 08:00

Tip: Schedules continue running even after page refresh!
